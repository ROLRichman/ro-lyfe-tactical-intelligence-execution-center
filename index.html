06 · Contracts / Legal Review / Execution

🧾 Investor-Grade Contract Checklist

This is a workflow checklist, not a substitute for attorney drafting or review. Since you said these agreements are being reviewed by a law firm, keep the legal language under counsel control before use.

Assignment protection
Confirm assignment rights and any notice/consent requirements.
Inspection / due-diligence window
Define access, inspection period, notice and termination mechanics.
Exit / termination clause
Use the attorney-approved language for permitted termination events.
Marketing rights
Define whether and how the interest may be marketed before closing.
Double-close fallback
Address a second closing only if counsel and the transaction structure permit it.
Title / lien / casualty / condemnation
Allocate due diligence and closing responsibilities.
Default / remedies
Keep remedies, deposits and cure periods attorney-approved.
Non-circumvention / confidentiality
Use only the language appropriate to the parties and transaction.
Closing costs / prorations
Identify who pays what and how adjustments are handled.
Broker / referral disclosures
Disclose compensation and relationships where required.
